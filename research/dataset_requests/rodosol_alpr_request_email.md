# RodoSol-ALPR request — email draft

RodoSol needs a **signed PDF**, not just an email. Steps:

1. Download the agreement from the dataset repository:
   `https://github.com/raysonlaroca/rodosol-alpr-dataset/blob/main/pdfs/license-agreement.pdf`
2. Print it. Under the first line, print your name and title
   (e.g. "`<Your Name>`, B.Tech student, `<Department>`, `<University>`").
3. Sign it **by hand** and date it. The form asks for a handwritten signature,
   so a typed name will not do.
4. Scan or photograph the signed page as a PDF.
5. Send it from your **university** email account, with the subject line exactly
   as below — the authors filter on it.

What you are agreeing to, in plain words: the data is provided as-is; you will
not redistribute, modify or use it commercially; its images may appear only in
academic publications and presentations; you indemnify the authors and the
Federal University of Paraná against damages from your use; and you cite their
VISAPP 2022 paper in any publication that uses it.

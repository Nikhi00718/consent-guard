# Surface: project page (docs/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. A visitor who has never heard of ConsentGuard decides whether to run it.

Audience: someone about to post a photo that has something in it they would rather not publish. Not a researcher. Constraints: the tool cannot run in a browser, so the page must prove the mechanism without pretending to be the app. Everything published must be safe to publish: the sample is synthetic, the numbers come from the committed scorecard.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page performs the product instead of advertising it. The visitor's first act is to move the line between the original photo and the file the tool saved. It refuses the category page: hero with two buttons, alternating screenshot-and-paragraph rows, numbered feature kickers, stat cards, and a table of everything that went right.

OWN-WORLD: the app's own world, unchanged. Warm charcoal table, near-black rebate strips for the header and the contact sheet, china-marker red-orange for the seam, the current mark and the one primary action. Archivo, self-hosted, no third-party requests. The redaction bar is used as typography: the headline covers its own words the way the tool covers a label.

STORY: drag the line, see a real before and after. Scan the contact sheet of four real screens. Read the scorecard of what the tool misses, published with the bad rows. Copy the two commands and run it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: rebate strip; headline at up to 4.75rem with "private parts" struck out in a solid black bar, one paragraph to its right; below it the reveal, full width, with the seam at the middle, film-edge labels at both ends and one line of caption. Actions sit under the reveal, not above it.

FORM: contact sheet and print, from the app's darkroom world. Signature interaction: the drag seam (range input, so pointer, touch and keyboard all work). Code-led. Chosen by the owner, 2026-09-20, from a review of the first version of this page.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Notes

- Kickers are banned here too: the only tracked-caps labels are film-edge markings on the reveal and the sheet.
- The scorecard is the page's loudest section on purpose. Publishing the failure rates is the product's position.
- Assets: `docs/assets/*.webp`, generated from real runs of the shipped build against the synthetic parcel photo (`scratchpad` generator, invented name, address, phone and courier). No dataset photo, no stock image, no third-party font.

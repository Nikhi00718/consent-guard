## Direction contract

THESIS: The reviewer is a darkroom light table for one photo. You mark what to hide the way a photographer marks a contact sheet with a china marker, and you leave with a finished print. It refuses the category default: navy dashboard, neon-teal glow, eyebrow labels, cards of stats.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-neutral charcoal table (#141312) so photo colour stays honest; a near-black film-rebate strip carrying the steps as frame markings in condensed caps; china-marker red-orange (#ff6a3d) reserved for marks, the current step and the one primary action; the cover itself is true black with marker hatching; the finished photo is a print with a paper-white border and a real drop shadow. Archivo with its width axis is the only UI face; system mono only for sizes and hashes.

STORY: Drop or paste a photo onto the easel, see the photo itself, press Erase and save (or check first). While it works, a marker scan line crosses the photo. Fix the cover with brush, eraser and loupe. Get the print, every check in plain words, and the reminder beside Download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rebate strip on top (brand, four frame steps, Start over). Left 5/12: two-tone headline at 48px, one paragraph, the "looks for" chips that are also the settings. Right 7/12: the easel, a large dark field with four marker crop corners, holding the drop target or the photo; the two actions sit directly under it.

FORM: Photo-editor familiarity with darkroom materials. Code-led (no image generation in this session). Signature interaction: the loupe and the true-size marker brush ring on the cover. Seed key: none (launcher not run; direction chosen by the owner from three presented).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
